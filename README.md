# Math Party! 🎉

A bright, vibrant cartoon addition & subtraction web game designed for an **iPad** and tailored for a **5-year-old**!

🌐 **Play Live on GitHub Pages**: https://craiz99.github.io/math-fun/

## Features

- **4 Rotating Animated Character Squads (Shuffled on Replay!)** — 3 questions each → 12-question victory run:
  1. **Panda Pals** — Giant Panda & Red Panda in an animated Bamboo Forest (with the **"I'm Big & Scary!" + Bamboo Rain** gag!)
  2. **Hero Chicken Crew** — Fierce Karate-Headband White Chicken & Golden Hen on an animated Sunny Farmyard (with the **comedic Raccoon ambush & aggressive Chicken chase-down!**)
  3. **JJ & Mikey** — JJ & Mikey the Turtle in an animated Adventure Park (with **Mikey's wrong-answer prank corrected by JJ!**)
  4. **Pop-Star Trio** — Rumi, Zoey & Mira on an animated Rainbow Concert Stage (with **Gwi-Ma boss arrival & Star-Power rainbow blast!**)
- **All-Characters "YOU WIN!!" Celebration Screen** with rainbow confetti, golden trophies, 12 stars, all 9 characters dancing together, and a giant **Play Again!** button returning to the Start screen.
- **Mixed Answer Inputs**: Standard **Calculator-Layout Keypad** (`7–8–9` top row, `4–5–6`, `1–2–3`, `⌫` / `0` / `✓`) mixed with big glossy **Multiple-Choice** jelly buttons.
- **Voice-Guided Interactive Hints for 5-Year-Olds**:
  - Tap **`🔊`** to hear any equation read aloud.
  - Tap **`💡 Hint`** for simple **Counting On / Counting Back** with big glowing tappable items that speak every count aloud, a **`🔊 Hear Again`** button, and a slow Step-2 animated walkthrough while background pop music gently ducks.
  - Tap **`🔄 New`** anytime to swap in a fresh problem with zero penalty.
- **Non-Repeating Math Engine (`≤ 20`)**:
  - 3 kid-friendly tiers (**Up to 10**, **Up to 15**, **Up to 20**) & 3 modes (**Add**, **Subtract**, **Mix!**)
  - Never repeats problems within or across consecutive sessions until the pool cycles.
- **Catchy Pop Music & Cartoon SFX**: Procedural Web Audio bubblegum-pop loop + cartoon sound effects + iPad-primed voiceover, with instant Music & Voice toggles.
