# Math Party! 🎉

A bright, vibrant cartoon addition & subtraction web game designed for an **iPad** and tailored for a **5-year-old who is good at math**!

🌐 **Play Live on GitHub Pages**: https://craiz99.github.io/math-fun/

## Features

- **4 Rotating Animated Character Squads** (3 questions each → 12-question victory run):
  1. **Panda Pals** — Giant Panda & Red Panda in the Sunny Bamboo Grove
  2. **Hero Chicken Crew** — Fierce Karate-Headband White Chicken & Golden Hen on the Sunny Farmyard, complete with a **comedic Raccoon ambush & aggressive Chicken counterattack!**
  3. **JJ & Mikey** — JJ & Mikey the Turtle in the Cartoon Adventure Park
  4. **Pop-Star Trio** — Rumi, Zoey & Mira on the Rainbow Concert Stage
- **All-Characters "YOU WIN!!" Celebration Screen** with rainbow confetti, golden trophy, 12 stars, and all 9 characters dancing together.
- **Mixed Answer Inputs**: Standard **Calculator-Layout Keypad** (`7-8-9` top row, `4-5-6`, `1-2-3`, `⌫` / `0` / `✓`) mixed with big glossy **Multiple-Choice** jelly buttons.
- **Educational Non-Repeating Math Engine**:
  - 3 selectable tiers (**Up to 15**, **Up to 20**, **Up to 50**) & 3 modes (**Add**, **Subtract**, **Mix!**)
  - Never repeats problems within or across consecutive sessions until the pool cycles
  - Progressive strategy hints teaching **Make a 10**, **Bridge Back to 10**, **Doubles**, tappable **Ten-Frames** / **Base-10 Blocks**, and Step-2 **Number-Line Hops**.
- **Catchy Pop Music & Cartoon SFX**: Procedural Web Audio bubblegum-pop loop + joyful sound effects + optional friendly voiceover, with instant Music & Voice toggles.
- **iPad-First UX**: Full-screen `100dvh` zero-scroll layout, large touch targets, no system keyboard popups, and Apple Web App "Add to Home Screen" support.
